# User anatomical correction

The user identified six limbs in the waiting animation: the two raised forepaws had been added while two grounded forelegs and two hindlegs remained. This was a real visual defect missed in the initial review.

The entire waiting row was regenerated and re-extracted. In the selected decoded/waiting.png, every pose has two raised forelegs and two supporting hindlegs, with only two ground paw endpoints and a continuous abdomen. Per-frame evidence is stored in rows/waiting/anatomy-review.json. The previous selected version is retained as decoded/waiting-v2-sixlimbs.png for the creation record.

All other standard animation rows were audited by tracing forelimbs from shoulders and hindlimbs from haunches to their paw endpoints. Waving and thinking preserve one raised forelimb, one planted forelimb and two hindlimbs. Chest/abdomen fur without a paw endpoint is not counted as an extra limb. The final look rows must also preserve exactly four anatomical limbs.
